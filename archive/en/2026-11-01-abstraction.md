Abstraction is fundamental to mathematics. Essentially, abstraction filters out specific details may differ between the objects of interest, and identifies core concepts that generalize many examples at once. One can identify the following seven levels of mathematical abstraction:
1. **Numbers:** Initially, we may care about quantities of "actual things", or length/area/volume of geometric shapes. Numbers can be assigned to any of these examples, and arithmetic operations "don't care" whether the numbers refer to apples, oranges, length of segments etc.
2. **Basic algebra:** Replaces concrete numbers with indeterminates (x, y etc.) that can have any numerical value, and studies equations written in those symbols.
3. **Functions:** Model quantities that depend on an indeterminate x. By elevating functions as the central object of study, we study all possible assignments to x at once---and how another quantity changes with respect to these assignments.
4. **Algebraic structures:** Concrete systems of linear equations are replaced by the abstract vector spaces of solutions to those systems; similarly, specific realizations of symmetries are replaced by the abstract groups that comprise possible symmetry operations.
5. **Sheaves:** Functions are replaces by "the structure of all possible functions" on a geometric space. Instead of studying the actual geometry, we study sheaves on the space.
6. **Classes:** Instead of concrete sets containing actual elements, we consider the formulae that the elements should satisfy.
7. **Categories:** Actual objects are replaced by the categories of objects and morphisms. The relative point of view tells us to study morphisms, rather than focus on the objects themselves. The categories themselves thus become the central object of study. 
8. **Sites, topoi and stacks:** Concrete topological spaces are replaced by categories that "look like" the open sets of a space; sheaves on such a space are replaced by pseudo-functors into the category of groupoids. Thus, instead of sheaves of sets on an actual geometric space, we get categories with category-valued sheaves.
9. **Higher categories:** Actual equalities between morphisms are replaced by isomorphisms satisfying certain coherence conditions. But these coherence diagrams also do not commute "on the nose"; rather, at every level, instead of equalities we have "higher arrows" that obey ever more convoluted coherence conditions, and in principle this goes on *ad infinitum*, so not a single concrete thing remains to hold on to.

Fun fact: each level of mathematical abstraction, when mastered, detached you further away from reality and corrupts your soul. If you've reached higher algebra then you are beyond salvation; you're essentially Voldemort.
